Hi! I’m Yukai Tang, a third-year PhD student in the Department of Operations Research and Financial Engineering at Princeton, where I’m fortunate to be coadvised by Prof. Amir Ali Ahmadi and Prof. Pravesh K. Kothari. I am broadly interested in optimization, particularly semidefinite programming.

Before coming to Princeton, I received my B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from Tsinghua University. In 2023, I had a great time working with Prof. Heng Yang at Harvard.
